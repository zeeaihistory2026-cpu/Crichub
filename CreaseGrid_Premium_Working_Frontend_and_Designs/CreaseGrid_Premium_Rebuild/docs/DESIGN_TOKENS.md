Design principles: compact navigation, text-forward reporting, meaningful charts and panels, selective imagery, strong information hierarchy, restrained visual effects and touch-friendly actions.

## Breakpoint considerations

- Desktop: 1440 and higher, full navigation.
- Medium: about 980, hamburger navigation and simplified multi-column layouts.
- Mobile: 390 and 375, two-up metric grids, sidebars hidden or transformed into top scrollable controls.
- Small phones: 320, single-column cards for particularly narrow content.
